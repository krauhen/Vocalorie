# meal-titling

## Purpose

TBD — capture the intent of AI-generated meal titles replacing app-side derivation from the first food item's name.

## Requirements

### Requirement: AI-generated meal title
The system SHALL have the nutrition-estimation model (`app/src/main/java/com/example/vocalorie/ai/KoogNutritionAgent.kt`) generate a short, natural-language title for the meal as part of its structured result (`title` field on `NutritionAgentResult` in `app/src/main/java/com/example/vocalorie/model/NutritionEstimateDtos.kt`), instead of the title being derived app-side from the first food item's name.

#### Scenario: Multi-item meal gets a synthesized title
- **WHEN** the user logs a meal that resolves to multiple distinct food items (e.g. "chicken breast, rice, and broccoli")
- **THEN** the AI result includes a short title summarizing the meal (e.g. "Chicken, Rice & Broccoli"), not just the first item's name

#### Scenario: Single-item meal still gets an AI title
- **WHEN** the user logs a meal that resolves to a single food item (e.g. "an apple")
- **THEN** the AI result includes a title for that item (e.g. "Apple"), generated by the model rather than copied verbatim from the query

### Requirement: AI title pre-fills the editor and remains user-editable
The system SHALL use the AI-generated title to pre-fill the meal editor's title field, and SHALL preserve the existing behavior of retaining a manually edited title on re-save unless it is effectively identical to the query text (`resolveMealTitle` in `app/src/main/java/com/example/vocalorie/data/MealMappers.kt`).

#### Scenario: AI title appears in the editor
- **WHEN** the user reviews a newly parsed meal in the editor
- **THEN** the title field is pre-filled with the AI-generated title

#### Scenario: Manual edit to the title is preserved
- **WHEN** the user edits the pre-filled title to a custom value and saves the meal
- **THEN** the custom title is stored and is not overwritten by the AI title on subsequent edits of that same meal
